`kgk_extensions.xtf` holds objects of the KGK models extending DMAV (KGK_Grundstuecke_V1_0,
KGK_Einzelobjekte_V1_0). It is cut from `models/DMAVTYM_Alles_V1_1/DMAV_Version_1_1.zip`: two
SelbstaendigesDauerndesRecht and three Einzelobjekt retagged as their KGK classes, one Liegenschaft, and
the objects they reference. ilivalidator 1.15.0 validates it against the KGK models of
https://models.kgk-cgc.ch.
